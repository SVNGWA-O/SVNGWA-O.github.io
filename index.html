eNotaryRW

A digital notarization platform for Rwanda, live in production at enotaryrw.com.
Why it's the flagship It takes something that used to take hours and makes it happen in minutes — live, serving real people in Rwanda today, and engineered to grow as demand does. It is the clearest picture of how I build: ship it for real, then make it scale.

Getting a document notarized in Rwanda could take hours. I built eNotaryRW to close that gap. Its Java matching engine pairs each client with an available private notary in real time, and it is built to scale: as demand skyrockets and thousands of requests hit at once, it keeps matching without latency climbing. The React frontend carries a single secure upload workflow, with an audit trail that makes any tampering evident.

A few decisions behind it
Built to scale Java's battle-tested concurrency keeps the matching engine pairing clients with notaries in real time even when traffic skyrockets — more users and requests do not push latency up.
An audit trail on every document Notarized documents carry legal weight. If one is ever disputed, the record has to account for everything that happened to the file, and when.
One secure upload workflow A single path for sensitive files is easier to test, audit, and keep correct than several.
